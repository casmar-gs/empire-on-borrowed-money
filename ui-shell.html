Empire on Borrowed Money

Lower East Side, 1924. You have 24 weeks to build a bigger fortune than the financier Cornelius Vane. Here's how the money works:

🎯
Win the race. End Week 24 with a higher net worth than Vane — the bar at the top. You start behind.
🤝
Mix your blocks. A shop earns +25% for each different shop you own next to it (up to +50%). Two of the same type next to each other? No bonus. Build variety.
🏙️
Don't flood a district. Each neighborhood has limited customers. Cram too many shops into one and they all earn less. Spread across districts.
💰
Borrow to grow. Cash alone is too slow — Vane will grab the best lots first. Leverage up while credit is calm. But watch the Credit Market: when froth runs red a crunch hits, and if you're over-extended it'll seize your shops. Keep a buffer.
