Empire on Borrowed Money

Lower East Side · 1924

Twenty-four weeks to build a bigger fortune than the financier Cornelius Vane. Here is how the money works:

Win the race. End Week XXIV with a higher net worth than Vane — the bar up top. You start behind.
Mix your blocks. A shop earns +25% for each different shop you own beside it (up to +50%). Two of the same kind side by side? Nothing. Build variety.
Don't flood a district. Each neighborhood has limited customers, shared with Vane. Cram too many shops in one and they all earn less. Spread out.
Borrow to grow. Cash alone is too slow — Vane grabs the best lots first. Lever up while credit is calm. But watch the Credit Market gauge: when it runs red a crunch hits, and the bank forecloses your weakest shops. Keep a buffer.
